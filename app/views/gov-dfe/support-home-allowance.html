Setting up home allowance

What is the setting up home allowance?

The setting up home allowance is a one-off payment from your local council to help you when you leave care.

You can use it to buy essentials for your new home, such as furniture or kitchen appliances.

It’s sometimes called the ‘Care Leavers Allowance’ or ‘Leaving Care Grant’.

Your personal advisor or someone from your local council can help you choose and buy what you need.

Who can get the setting up home allowance

You can get the setting up home allowance if your care status is ‘former relevant child’.

  • are aged between 18 and 25
  • spent more than 13 weeks in care since the age of 14, including after you turned 16

Being in care means you were:

  • in foster care
  • in residential care or similar
  • in supported lodgings
  • under special guardianship
  • in custody under the age of 18, for example in a secure children's home, secure training centre, or young offender institution
  • an unaccompanied asylum-seeking child

If you’re a ‘relevant child’ or ‘eligible child’, you’ll be entitled to the grant when you turn 18.

To check if you're eligible, ask your personal advisor or local council to confirm your care leaver status.

The setting up home allowance is for people in England. If you live in Scotland or Wales, different support is available.

  • Scotland: Care Leaver Payment
  • Wales: Basic Income for Care Leavers

How much you'll get

You’ll get at least £3,000.

Your local council may give you more, depending on your individual circumstances.

How and when you’ll get it

You can apply for the setting up home allowance when you turn 18.

Your personal advisor or someone from your local council will help you with this.

Once the council agrees on the amount, they’ll pay it into your bank account.

You may get the full amount all at once or in smaller payments over time. This depends on your local council and personal advisor.

If you’re in a 'stay put' arrangement, you can apply at 18 but choose to receive the payment later, for example when you move out or turn 21.

If you’re getting other support

You’re entitled to the setting up home allowance even if you’re getting other financial support, such as benefits, or living in supported accommodation or elsewhere.

If you have issues with your allowance

Speak to your personal advisor or someone from your local council if you’re not receiving all the support you need or want to make a complaint.

More information

Contact your local council to find out more about the setting up home allowance and check if you’re entitled to a council tax exemption under the ‘Local Offer’.
